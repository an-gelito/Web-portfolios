How we estimate

  1. We find comparable sales

    Homes of similar size and age that sold near yours in the past several months.

  2. We adjust for differences

    An extra bathroom, a bigger lot, a new roof. Each one moves the number up or down.

  3. We check current listings

    What buyers can choose from right now affects what yours can ask. Your agent walks you through it.

An estimate is a range, not a promise. A walk-through of the home is the only way to firm it up, and we only schedule one if you ask.

Questions people ask first

Does it cost anything?

No. The estimate is free and there is no obligation to list with us.

Will someone call me right away?

Only if you choose "Within 3 months" in step 2. Otherwise you get the estimate by email and we leave it there.

How is this different from an online estimate tool?

A person reads the comparable sales and checks that they make sense for your street. Automated tools can miss things like a recent renovation.

What do you do with my address?

We use it to prepare your estimate. We don't sell it or share it with other agents.

How long does it take?

You'll have the written estimate within one business day.

    Before you book

    Do I need to be home?

    No. Many customers leave a key or a door code. We will confirm how to get in when we confirm your booking.

    Do you bring your own supplies?

    Yes. If you prefer a specific product, tell us and we will use it.

    I have pets. Is that a problem?

    Not at all. Let us know about them in your booking so we can plan around them.

    Is the estimate the final price?

    The estimate is based on what you entered. If the home turns out to be very different, we will tell you before we start.

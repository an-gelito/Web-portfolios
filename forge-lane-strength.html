Six weeks to your first real squat

Foundations is a beginner strength program for adults who have never trained with a barbell. Three 50-minute sessions a week, coached in small groups.

Week by week, the plates go on.

Four lifts. That is the whole plan

Beginners get strong fastest by doing a few movements well and often. You will learn these four.

  • SquatSit between your hips, keep your chest up, stand up with the floor.
  • DeadliftPick a bar up from the floor with a flat back and tight lats.
  • PressPush the bar overhead while your legs and core stay locked in.
  • RowPull the bar to your belly and keep your ribs down.

What the six weeks look like

    Pick your three days

      Learn one rep at a time

      Week one starts with the empty bar. A coach counts the tempo out loud until the movement is boring, then we add weight.

      • The bar stays over the middle of your foot
      • Down for two seconds, up for one
      • Chest up, heels flat on the floor

      Is this for you

      A good fit

      • You have never trained with a barbell, or you are coming back after a long break.
      • You want someone to show you the movements and check them.
      • You can commit to three sessions a week for six weeks.

      Not the right program yet

      • You are preparing for a lifting competition.
      • You have an injury and have not been cleared to train by your doctor.
      • You want a different workout every day.

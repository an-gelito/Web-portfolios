What a cleaning does

We remove plaque and tartar, polish each tooth, and check between them with floss. Most people feel the difference before they leave the chair.

  • Plaque comes off
  • Teeth are polished smooth
  • We check between every tooth

Your first visit takes about an hour

Here is what happens, in order.

  1. We talk first

    10 minutes

    We hear what brought you in, any worries, and your dental history.

  2. A gentle exam

    25 minutes

    We check teeth, gums, and bite, and show you what we see on a screen.

  3. X-rays if you need them

    10 minutes

    Only the ones that help us decide. We skip them if you have recent ones.

  4. A plan in writing

    15 minutes

    You leave with next steps, costs, and options. No pressure to book anything that day.

Questions people ask before they book

Do you take my insurance?

We work with most major plans. Tell us your plan when you request a visit and we will check your benefits before you arrive.

My child is nervous. What can we do?

Bring them in for a hello visit first. They can sit in the chair, meet the team, and leave without any treatment.

What if I have a toothache?

Call us the moment it starts. We keep space in each day for urgent visits.

How do I change or cancel?

Reply to your confirmation or call us. We ask for one business day's notice so someone else can take the slot.

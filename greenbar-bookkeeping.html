What happens each month

  1. We pull your records

    Bank and card feeds come in on the 1st. You send receipts and invoices as photos or PDFs.

  2. We match and categorize

    Every line is matched to a document. Anything we can't match goes on a short list for you.

  3. You answer the short list

    Usually a handful of questions, like "What was this $212 charge on the 14th?"

  4. You get your page

    By the 10th: profit for the month, what customers owe you, and what is due next.

Questions people ask first

I'm several months behind. Is that a problem?

No. The Catch-up plan exists for this. We start with the oldest month and work forward, then tell you what is left.

Do you do taxes?

No. We prepare clean records and hand them to your tax preparer, which usually shortens their work.

What do I need to send you?

Read-only access to your bank and card accounts, plus receipts and invoices. We never move money.

What if you can't match a charge?

It goes on the short list in step 3. We don't guess and we don't leave it out.

Can I stop any time?

Yes. The monthly plans have no contract. You keep your books and the files we made.
